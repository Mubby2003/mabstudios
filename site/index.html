06 — About

Hello — we are
MAB Studios

We are a photography studio based in the United Kingdom, working across portraits, weddings, traditional ceremonies and events. Our lead photographer shoots on Nikon Z and is part of the Nikon Z Creator programme.

Much of what we do is Nigerian and British at once — a white gown in the morning and aso oke in the afternoon, a register office and a gele, all in the same day. We light both properly, and we know the order the day runs in.
